# Design brief

- **Product:** independent e-commerce interface concept for digital pianos, prepared as a web-development portfolio demo.
- **Primary audience:** international clients reviewing a storefront prototype; secondary audience: people exploring the demo.
- **Primary outcome:** explore a sample catalog, compare models, hear generated tones, try the room-fit tool, and inspect a simulated cart flow.
- **Voice:** clear, confident, music-aware English. Avoid sales claims that cannot be proven.
- **Principles:** disclose demo status immediately; make interactions useful; do not invent trust signals; preserve the existing premium visual identity.
- **Constraints:** not affiliated with or endorsed by Roland. Prices, stock, reviews, offers, and store policies are sample content. No real checkout, contact submission, or newsletter signup.
- **Existing direction:** charcoal/black surfaces, warm brass accent, cream text, Unbounded display face, Manrope body face, live keyboard and product-card interaction.
- **Success:** polished, responsive demo that loads reliably, demonstrates real front-end interactions, and never appears to accept a real order.
